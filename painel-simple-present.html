Reading

Leia o texto sobre a rotina da Ana e responda às perguntas.

Ana is a student. She wakes up at 6:30 every morning and takes a quick shower. After that, she brushes her teeth and has breakfast with her family. At 7:15, she leaves home and goes to school by bike. Classes start at 8 a.m. and finish at noon. In the afternoon, Ana does her homework and studies for tests. She usually watches a bit of TV before dinner. Her family has dinner together at 7 p.m. and talks about the day. Later, Ana reads a book or talks to her friends online. She goes to bed at 10 p.m. because she needs eight hours of sleep.
