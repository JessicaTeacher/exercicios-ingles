There to be

There is / There are — exist, present

Reading — Julia's Apartment

Julia lives in a small apartment in Rio de Janeiro. There is a cozy living room with a big yellow sofa. There are two bedrooms and one bathroom. In the kitchen, there is a small table and four chairs. There aren't any pets in the apartment, but there are many plants near the window. Is there a balcony? Yes, there is! Julia loves to relax there in the evening.

Comprehension

1. How many bedrooms are there in Julia's apartment?

2. Is there a balcony in the apartment?

3. Are there any pets in the apartment?

4. What color is the sofa?

Listening — Fill in the gap

Clique no alto-falante para ouvir a frase completa e depois preencha a lacuna com: is / are / isn't / aren't / Is / Are.

there many books on the shelf?

Verbs of Rotina

Daily routine — wake up, have breakfast, go to work…

Reading — Tom's Routine

Tom usually wakes up at 7 o'clock. He has breakfast and then goes to work — he is a teacher at a primary school. He starts work at 8.30 and his classes begin at 9 o'clock. Each class is one hour: three in the morning and two in the afternoon. He finishes at 4 o'clock. After work, he sometimes goes to the gym or goes home and cooks dinner. In the evenings he likes to read a book or watch TV, or he takes his dog for a walk. His dog is a chocolate brown Labrador named Charlie, who is four years old. Tom enjoys his routine, even though it keeps him busy.

Answer the questions

1. What time does Tom wake up?

2. Where does he work?

3. What time does his class start?

4. What does he do after work?

5. What's his dog's name?

6. What color is his dog?
